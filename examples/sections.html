Harbour log, in sections

Monday

Calm water from first light. Three boats out before six, all back by noon with a fair catch.

Tide

High water at 07:40, low at 13:55. The north channel stayed clear all day.

Gauge reading

07:40  4.1 m
13:55  0.9 m

The weather turned by Wednesday.

Tuesday

A light westerly. The slipway was repainted, and the lamp on the outer mole was replaced.

Work done

  • Slipway: two coats, dry by evening.
  • Outer lamp: new lens, tested at dusk.

The rest of the day was quiet. Nets were mended on the quay, and the ferry ran to time in both directions.

In the afternoon a school class visited the lifeboat house and was shown the launch from the gallery.

Wednesday

Wind backing south-west and rising through the morning. No boats went out after nine.

Gale warning

Force 8 expected by evening. Moorings were doubled and the ferry stayed in.

Moorings checked

All twelve, twice: at noon and again at four.
